# GRC Cybersecurity Portfolio

This is my cybersecurity GRC portfolio.

I built these projects to practice the kind of work I would expect to do in a GRC / IT Risk / IT Audit role: reviewing controls, working with evidence, recording risks and findings, and tracking remediation.

Everything in this repository is **simulated, lab-based or educational**. The companies, users, vendors, evidence and results are fictional.

## What is covered

**Risk Management · ISO 27001 · ITGC · TPRM · Privacy · IAM · Cloud GRC · AI Governance**

The projects also use Excel and basic reporting to keep the work easy to review.

## What you will find in the projects

Depending on the project, the folder includes things such as:

- Excel workpapers and dashboards
- Control matrices
- Risk registers
- Evidence indexes
- Findings logs
- Remediation trackers
- Assessment / testing workpapers
- Executive summaries
- Methodology notes
- Diagrams and supporting documentation

## How I approached the projects

I tried to keep the same basic flow across the portfolio:

**Requirement / Risk → Control → Evidence → Assessment → Finding → Remediation → Validation**

That is the part I want the portfolio to show, rather than just a list of frameworks or certificates.

## Tools and topics

**Frameworks:** ISO 27001:2022, NIST CSF, NIST AI RMF, CIS Controls, DPDP, GDPR, ISO 42001 concepts

**GRC work:** Risk assessment, ITGC, control testing, TPRM, privacy, IAM governance, cloud GRC, AI governance

**Tools:** Microsoft Excel, GitHub, and documented workflows that can be carried into Power Query, Power BI or a GRC platform like ServiceNow

## A note about the numbers

The numbers in the projects are part of the fictional case studies. They are there to make the workbooks and reports realistic enough to practice with; they are not claims about real clients or employers.
