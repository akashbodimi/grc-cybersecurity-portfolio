# Sample DPIA — Product Analytics

> Fictional processing activity. This is a portfolio workpaper, not a legal determination.

## Processing

Northstar uses product analytics to understand feature usage, identify usability issues and improve service performance. The simulated data set contains pseudonymous user identifiers, usage events and device/browser metadata.

## Why a DPIA is Being Considered

The processing involves behavioural/usage analytics at scale and may create risks to individual privacy when identifiers, event-level data and third-party analytics services are combined. GDPR Article 35 is used as a reference point for deciding when high-risk processing requires a DPIA.

## Necessity & Proportionality

Questions documented for review:
1. What exact product decisions require each field?
2. Can the same objective be achieved with fewer fields?
3. Can identifiers be separated or shortened?
4. Are optional analytics clearly distinguished from core service operations?
5. Are retention periods proportionate to the analytics purpose?

## Key Risks

| Risk | Assessment | Proposed Safeguard |
|---|---|---|
| Excessive event collection | High | Field-level minimisation review |
| Optional processing without clear choice | High | Preference management and withdrawal testing |
| Excessive retention | High | 12-month raw-event target and automated lifecycle control |
| Internal access | Medium | Quarterly access recertification |
| Processor/subprocessor exposure | Medium | TPRM and contract/evidence review |
| Cross-border processing | Medium | Document processing locations and applicable transfer requirements |

## Residual Assessment

The simulation retains **Medium residual privacy risk** until the planned minimisation, withdrawal, retention and processor-review safeguards are implemented and validated.

## Reassessment Triggers

Reopen the DPIA when there is:
- Material change in data categories or collection
- New analytics provider or subprocessor
- Significant increase in processing scale
- New profiling or automated decision use
- New processing location or transfer arrangement
- Material security/privacy incident
