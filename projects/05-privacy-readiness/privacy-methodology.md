# Privacy Readiness Methodology

> Simulated portfolio methodology for Northstar Cloud Services Pvt. Ltd. No real personal data is used.

## Objective

Assess privacy governance readiness across customer, employee, applicant, vendor, analytics and website processing using a practical GRC approach.

## Framework Coverage

The project uses:
- India: Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025
- EU: GDPR concepts for an organization with EU-facing processing
- Operational privacy controls: data inventory, notice/transparency, consent or other processing basis, data minimisation, rights handling, processor governance, retention/deletion, incident response and DPIA

The DPDP Rules 2025 were notified on 14 November 2025 with phased commencement dates. The portfolio therefore distinguishes current readiness activities from obligations whose notified commencement occurs later. See the source list for the official Gazette references.

## Assessment Lifecycle

**Identify Processing → Map Data → Determine Purpose/Basis → Assess Risk → DPIA Where Appropriate → Review Rights & Transparency → Review Retention & Deletion → Assess Processors → Record Gaps → Remediate → Validate**

## Privacy Risk Scoring

Each risk is assessed using:
- Data Impact: 1–5
- Likelihood: 1–5

**Inherent Risk Score = Data Impact × Likelihood**

| Score | Tier |
|---:|---|
| 1–5 | Low |
| 6–11 | Medium |
| 12–19 | High |
| 20–25 | Critical |

This is an internal portfolio prioritisation method, not a statutory risk formula.

## DPIA Approach

A DPIA should document the processing, assess necessity and proportionality, assess risks to individuals and identify safeguards. Under GDPR Article 35, a DPIA is required before processing likely to result in a high risk to individuals' rights and freedoms; the exact applicability depends on the processing context.

## Rights Handling

The tracker demonstrates operational handling of access, correction/rectification, erasure and consent-withdrawal requests. The actual scope, deadlines, exemptions and identity-verification process must be determined for the applicable legal framework.

## Retention

Retention is tied to business purpose, legal/business hold and deletion method. Portfolio dates are simulated operational targets and are not legal retention advice.

## Important Limitation

This project demonstrates privacy GRC workflow and evidence discipline. It is not legal advice, a data-protection compliance certification, or a determination that any real organization complies with DPDP or GDPR.
