# Sample Privacy Notice — Northstar Cloud Services

> Fictional draft for portfolio demonstration only. It is not a legal notice.

## What information we process

Depending on the service you use, Northstar may process identifiers, business contact information, account details, support content, product-usage information, device/browser information, billing references and communication preferences.

## Why we process it

We process personal data to provide and secure the service, manage accounts, respond to support requests, process billing, improve the product, communicate about services and maintain security.

Optional analytics and marketing activities should be presented separately where consent or another applicable legal requirement calls for user choice.

## Your choices and rights

Northstar should provide an accessible channel for applicable data-protection requests, including correction/updating, deletion where applicable, access or other rights required under the relevant framework.

Where consent is relied upon, withdrawal should be reasonably easy and the organisation should document the effect of withdrawal on downstream processing.

## Retention

Personal data should be kept only for as long as necessary for the documented purpose, subject to applicable legal, contractual or legitimate business requirements and approved holds.

## Service providers

Northstar may use technology providers to process personal data on its behalf. The privacy governance process should maintain a current processor/subprocessor inventory and appropriate contractual and assurance records.

## Security

Northstar should apply appropriate technical and organisational safeguards, including access controls, authentication, encryption where appropriate, monitoring and incident response.
