## Validation criteria

A remediation action should not be considered closed merely because someone says it is complete. Closure evidence should demonstrate:

1. The control/process was implemented.
2. The relevant owner approved it.
3. Evidence is dated and traceable.
4. The control operated for an appropriate period where applicable.
5. A reviewer validated the remediation.
