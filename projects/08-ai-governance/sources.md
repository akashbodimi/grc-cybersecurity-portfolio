# AI Governance Sources

References accessed September 2026.

1. NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0  
https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework  
NIST describes AI RMF as a voluntary framework for managing risks to individuals, organizations and society. NIST also notes the framework is being revised.

2. NIST AI RMF Generative AI Profile (NIST AI 600-1)  
https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-generative-artificial-intelligence-profile  
The profile provides AI RMF-oriented guidance for generative AI risk management.

3. ISO/IEC 42001:2023  
https://www.iso.org/standard/42001  
ISO describes 42001 as requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining and continually improving an AI management system.

4. EU AI Act — Regulation (EU) 2024/1689  
https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj  
The regulation establishes harmonised rules for AI and has phased application dates; the current consolidated version is published by EUR-Lex.

5. NIST AI Resource Center  
https://airc.nist.gov/  
Operational resources for AI RMF implementation, including profiles and testing/evaluation resources.

> Source notes are for educational framework mapping. This project does not provide legal advice or a regulatory conclusion.
