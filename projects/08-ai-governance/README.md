# AI Governance Risk & Control Assessment

**Status:** Planned — simulated organization

## Objective

Assess an organization's use of AI from governance, security, privacy and operational-risk perspectives.

## Include

- AI system inventory
- Use-case risk classification
- Data/privacy risk
- Security and misuse risk
- Human oversight
- Vendor/model risk
- Monitoring
- Incident handling
- AI policy requirements

## Framework references

- NIST AI RMF
- ISO/IEC 42001 concepts
- Applicable privacy and information-security requirements

## Deliverables

- AI inventory
- AI risk register
- Control matrix
- AI acceptable-use policy
- Impact assessment template
- Executive summary

> This is a simulated governance assessment, not an independent legal or regulatory determination.
