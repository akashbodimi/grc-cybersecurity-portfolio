# Third-Party Risk Assessment

**Status:** Planned — simulated vendor assessment

## Objective

Assess a fictional technology vendor before onboarding and demonstrate the TPRM lifecycle.

## Lifecycle

**Intake → Criticality → Questionnaire → Evidence Review → Risk Scoring → Remediation → Approval/Exception → Reassessment**

## Evidence examples

- ISO 27001 certificate / SoA
- SOC 2 report
- Security policies
- Pen-test summary
- Incident response summary
- Business continuity evidence
- Privacy terms
- Subprocessor information

## Deliverables

- Vendor profile
- Security questionnaire
- Evidence review
- Vendor risk score
- Open findings
- Remediation plan
- Risk decision memo
