# IAM Governance Reference Sources

Public references used to structure the simulated control themes:

- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 — Account Management (AC-2), Separation of Duties (AC-5), Least Privilege (AC-6), Identification and Authentication themes: https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/detail/sp/800-53/rev-5/final
- NIST SP 800-63B — Digital Identity Guidelines: Authentication and Lifecycle Management: https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-3/sp800-63b.html
- ISO/IEC 27001 — information security management systems and Annex A access/identity themes: https://www.iso.org/standard/27001
- CIS Controls v8 — Account Management and Access Control Management themes: https://www.cisecurity.org/controls/v8

> Control wording in the portfolio is paraphrased. The project is a simulated educational exercise, not a standards certification or legal opinion.
