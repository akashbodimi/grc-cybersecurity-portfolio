# Sample Workpaper — Periodic Access Review

> Simulated portfolio work. No real user accounts or organizational data are used.

## Objective

Determine whether sampled application access was reviewed by an authorized owner and whether retain/remove decisions were supported by evidence.

## Population

12 simulated user-access records across CRM, ITSM and Finance.

## Test Procedure

1. Confirm the population source and review period.
2. Confirm each user has an identifiable role.
3. Verify owner decision.
4. Trace removal/modification decisions to evidence.
5. Record exceptions requiring follow-up validation.

## Results

10 samples were retained without exception. Two samples resulted in exceptions:

- **AR-005:** Finance AP access marked for removal; closure evidence pending.
- **AR-010:** CRM access no longer required after role change; removal validation pending.

## Conclusion

The simulated control operated with exceptions requiring closure evidence. The review process exists, but exception closure should be tracked to verified completion rather than ending at the owner decision.
